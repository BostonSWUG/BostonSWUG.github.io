About the Festival

Engineering community meets summer celebration

The BostonSWUG Festival began in 2023 as a celebration of community and engineering craft. What started as a party has grown into the region's premier free half-day event for SolidWorks engineers, designers, and power users.

This year's 4th Annual edition brings a sharpened focus on AI in engineering — with a dedicated panel, hands-on labs, and practical sessions on the tools reshaping how we design. No cost, no catch. Just a great afternoon with your people.

Sessions

What you'll learn

Confirmed sessions below. More being added — check back soon.

Panel Discussion
AI in Engineering
Dan Herzberg · Joe Lance · Austin Etter + more
A candid panel on how AI is changing the engineering workflow — design automation, DFM, connected teams, and where the tools are actually useful vs. overhyped.
Product Demo
Practical AI for the Modern SolidWorks Designer
Austin Etter — Bananaz.ai
Live demos of DFM checks, 3D cost-reduction analysis, intelligent drawing review checklists, async 2D/3D design reviews, and visual comparison for design change validation.
Technical Talk · 50 min
Consistency by Design
Stephanie Hayna — ICU Medical / SWUG NYC
Best practices for regulated environments — repeatable validated design processes, compliance-ready workflows, and techniques for standardization across engineering teams in medtech.
Hands-On Workshop
FAB Lab Session
Chinloo Lama + Sal Lama — Dassault Systèmes
