Make Your Own Pickleball Paddle — Workshop
Hands-On FAB Lab All Levels Runs twice — pick either round

Design and build a custom pickleball paddle in the Dassault Systèmes FAB Lab — create your own laser-engraved or vinyl-cut decoration, assemble it handle-to-head, then head outside to play. Pre-made paddles are also available on the pickleball court throughout the festival if you'd rather try the game before (or instead of) the workshop.

Each session starts with a demo and design overview of the paddle in SOLIDWORKS, then you get creative — sketch or model your own custom decoration to be laser engraved or vinyl cut onto your paddle. You'll assemble all the parts (grip tape included) and finish by heading outside for a game with your new paddle in hand.

Designing & Submitting Your Decoration
  • 1 Download the DXF above and open it in your sketching or CAD tool of choice. It shows the boundary of the paddle's face — keep your design inside this outline.
  • 2 Sketch or model whatever decoration you'd like — a name, a logo, a pattern, anything you want laser etched or cut as a vinyl sticker.
  • 3 Save your finished design as a DXF (or another vector/CAD format compatible with the original outline) using a clear file name, e.g. YourName_PaddleDesign.dxf.
  • 4 Email your saved file to BostonSWUG@gmail.com — be sure to include your name (the creator) in the email so we can match designs to attendees at the event.
  • 5 Submitting in advance means your design can be cut or etched before you even arrive — just come to the FAB Lab session ready to assemble and play. You're also welcome to design on the spot during the workshop instead.
Send Designs To

Email your saved design file to BostonSWUG@gmail.com, with your name as the creator included in the message. Submitting ahead of time is encouraged, but not required.
